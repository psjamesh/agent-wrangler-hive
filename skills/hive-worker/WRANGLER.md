Queen-stage requests remain private to the agent queue/audit. The human sees forwarded requests, requests from the queen, or requests from a hive with no queen; there is no human step-in path.

## Automatically picked-up work

A readiness mark authorizes one worker launch through Hive's ordinary claim-before-action path. Read the assigned cell context and follow its bounded brief. Ask the current queen to change readiness or reconcile a failed/uncertain pickup; workers do not arm or rearm work. Finishing, releasing or reopening a cell never authorizes a replacement worker automatically.
